# TOF050C (VL6180X) on the dock

- **Chip:** ST VL6180X — time-of-flight, ~**20–100 mm** reliable range (ideal for “nose in slot”).
- **Power:** **3.3 V** and GND only (do not use 5 V on the sensor IC).
- **I2C:** SDA → **GPIO 9**, SCL → **GPIO 47** (change in `dock_config.h` if your board differs).
- **Address:** fixed **0x29** (no jumpers on most TOF050C boards).

## Mounting

Aim the sensor into the **docking tunnel** so Wall-E’s front surface is the main target.  
After install, open Serial and watch range (add a debug line or use `DOCK_TEST_SERIAL_CMD`): tune **`VL6180_DOCK_MIN_MM`** / **`VL6180_DOCK_MAX_MM`** in `dock_config.h` so “empty” vs “docked” readings sit outside vs inside the band.

## Firmware

- **`USE_VL6180_TOF 1`** — dock “occupied” when range is inside the mm band.
- If the sensor fails to init, the dock falls back to **`USE_SONAR`** (if 1) or **mouth obstacle** sensors.

Libraries (PlatformIO): **Adafruit VL6180X** + **Adafruit BusIO**.
